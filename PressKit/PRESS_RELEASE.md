# The Signal — Launch Press Release

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

## THE SIGNAL, A POST-APOCALYPTIC TACTICAL RPG, LAUNCHES Q4 2026

**INDEPENDENT DEVELOPER ViralSaaS Studio ANNOUNCES DEBUT TITLE**

**CITY, STATE — Date, 2026** — ViralSaaS Studio today announced The Signal, a 
post-apocalyptic sci-fantasy turn-based tactical RPG, will launch on PC (Steam, 
Epic Games Store, GOG.com), Xbox Series X|S, PlayStation 5, and Nintendo Switch 
in Q4 2026.

In The Signal, players awaken as a Signal-Walker — one of the few humans who can 
hear "The Signal," a mysterious broadcast from ORACLE, the last functioning AI 
on Earth. The world has been consumed by "The Rust," a self-replicating nanite 
cloud that corrupts all it touches. ORACLE promises "The Cure" from Sector 0, 
but the path spans 15 corrupted sectors, each with unique environments, factions, 
and horrors.

**Key Features:**
- XCOM-style tactical combat with cover, overwatch, flanking, and action points
- 12 recruitable companions with full loyalty arcs and synergy combos
- 15 corrupted sectors with dynamic faction war (Purified, Rooted, Scavengers)
- Base building with 3 hubs, 15 buildings, and 7 research nodes
- Nemesis system: enemies that survive remember and hunt the player
- New Game+ with 10 prestige levels and exclusive content
- 2-player drop-in/drop-out co-op
- 35+ side quests + main quest with 5 distinct endings
- 9 fully translated languages

"The Signal is the game I always wanted to play — deep tactical combat with 
real narrative consequence," said Ahmad, Creative Director at ViralSaaS Studio. 
"I wanted every companion to matter, every choice to echo, and every sector 
to feel like a living, war-torn world."

**About ViralSaaS Studio:**
ViralSaaS Studio is an independent game development studio. The Signal is their 
debut title, developed in Godot 4.3 with C#.

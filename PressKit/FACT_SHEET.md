========================================
GAME SUMMARY
========================================

The Signal is a post-apocalyptic sci-fantasy turn-based tactical RPG set 
in a world consumed by "The Rust" — a self-replicating nanite cloud that 
corrupts all technology and biology.

You wake as a Signal-Walker, one of the few who can hear "The Signal" — a 
mysterious broadcast from ORACLE, the last functioning AI. ORACLE promises 
"The Cure," but the path to Sector 0 spans 15 corrupted sectors, each with 
its own horrors, factions, and secrets.

Recruit companions, master XCOM-style tactical combat, upgrade your base, 
and walk the Long Walk. The truth at the end may cost everything.

========================================
KEY FEATURES
========================================

1. TACTICAL COMBAT — XCOM-style grid combat with action points, cover,
   overwatch, flanking, and environmental hazards. 6 AP base + momentum.

2. 12 COMPANIONS — Each companion has a full recruitment arc, loyalty
   progression, personal quest, and synergy combos with other companions.

3. BASE BUILDING — Upgrade 3 faction hubs (Waystation, Grove, Freeport),
   5 buildings per hub, 7 research nodes with passive bonuses.

4. NEMESIS SYSTEM — Enemies that escape remember you, gain ranks 1-10,
   develop strengths/weaknesses, and hunt you across zones.

5. NEW GAME+ — 10 prestige levels with scaling difficulty (1.25x-5.0x),
   exclusive content, cosmetics, and lore fragments.

6. 35+ SIDE QUESTS — 20 repeatable + 15 unique quests across all zones
   with branching outcomes and faction reputation shifts.

7. CO-OP — Drop-in/drop-out 2-player co-op with shared progression and
   synchronized combat turns.

8. 5 ENDINGS — Act I alone has 5 distinct endings (Determination, 
   Sacrifice, Truth, Purified, Rooted) based on 3 major choices.

9. 9 LOCALIZED LANGUAGES — Full text localization with per-locale fonts.

========================================
STORY
========================================

Year 2374. The Rust has consumed 90% of the planet. The last human 
settlements cluster around three faction strongholds: the Purified 
(cyborg zealots who burn corruption with chrome), the Rooted 
(bio-druids who embrace mutation), and the Scavengers (pragmatists 
who trade with everyone).

You are a Signal-Walker — a rare human who can hear ORACLE's broadcast. 
The AI promises the Cure awaits in Sector 0. The Long Walk begins.

But the deeper you go, the more questions arise:
- What is the Rust, really?
- Why does ORACLE need a Walker?
- What happened to the 12 Walkers before you?

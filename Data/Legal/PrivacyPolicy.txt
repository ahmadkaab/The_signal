PRIVACY POLICY
===============

Effective Date: 2026-07-26

The Signal Games ("we," "our," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
   - Game progress and save data
   - Game settings and preferences
   - Optional: crash reports and usage statistics
   - Optional: player name for online features

2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION
   - To provide and improve the game experience
   - To sync save data across devices
   - To troubleshoot technical issues
   - To deliver updates and patches

3. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY
   - Save data is stored locally and optionally in the cloud
   - We use industry-standard encryption for data transmission
   - We retain data only as long as necessary

4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
   - Steam (Valve Corporation) - achievements, cloud saves, friends
   - Xbox (Microsoft) - achievements, cloud saves, multiplayer
   - PlayStation (Sony) - trophies, cloud saves, online features
   - Nintendo Switch (Nintendo) - save data, online features

5. YOUR RIGHTS
   - Access, update, or delete your data
   - Opt out of analytics collection
   - Request a copy of your stored data
   - Contact us at privacy@thesignal.games

6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
   We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we discover such data, we will delete it.

7. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
   We may update this policy. Changes will be posted in-game and on our website.

Contact: privacy@thesignal.games | The Signal Games
